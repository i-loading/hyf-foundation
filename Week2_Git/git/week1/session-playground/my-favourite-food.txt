Easy Fluffy Omelette

Ingredients

2–3 eggs
1 tbsp milk or water
Pinch of salt
Black pepper
1 tsp butter
Optional fillings: grated cheese, ham, mushrooms, tomatoes, spinach, or herbs

Method

Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add milk, salt, and pepper, then whisk until well combined.
Melt the butter in a non-stick frying pan over medium-low heat.
Pour in the eggs. As they begin to set, gently push the cooked edges toward the centre, letting uncooked egg flow underneath.
When the omelette is mostly set but still slightly moist on top, add your fillings to one half.
Fold the other half over and cook for another 30–60 seconds.
Slide onto a plate and serve immediately.

Tip: For a soft, fluffy omelette, avoid high heat and don't overcook the eggs
